Seven Keep — Delete your account

Seven Keep, by The Gate Keeper

In the game

  1. Open Settings (the cog, top right of the home screen).
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Read what is deleted and tap Delete forever.

You need an internet connection. The game then starts again as a new guest.

What is deleted

  • your save in your game account: level, experience, coins, gems, Energy, cards and cosmetics, orders, campaign, Gauntlet and season progress, achievements;
  • your online results, your hidden skill ratings and your player identifier;
  • the game account itself (Unity Gaming Services).

On the device, the game's save, friends list and settings are reset (language, sound and emotes are kept). A duel in progress is counted as a loss for you.

Gems and items bought with real money are deleted with the save and are not refunded. Remove Ads and the other one-time purchases stay with your Google account and are restored when you play again.

Analytics data sent to GameAnalytics, if you allowed it, is not linked to your game account; to ask for its deletion, contact us (below).

Delete some data, keep your account

  • Analytics: turn off Settings → Personalised ads and analytics (in the EEA / UK: Analytics) and nothing more is sent to GameAnalytics. To delete what it already received, email us (below) with the subject Delete my Seven Keep analytics data.
  • Advertising: in the EEA / UK, Settings → Ad privacy choices changes your answer to the ad partners at any time; anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Your display name: change it at any time in Settings; the old one is no longer shown.
  • Online results and ratings: email us with the subject Delete my Seven Keep online results and your display name; we delete your match history and hidden ratings and keep your progress.

We act on an emailed request within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted: the data you asked about: the analytics events GameAnalytics holds for this installation, or your online match history and hidden skill ratings. What is kept: your game account, your progress, your purchases and your display name. Deleted data is not kept afterwards by us; GameAnalytics and Unity may hold it in their backups for the periods their own privacy policies give.

Without the game

If you no longer have the game installed, email tgkgames@protonmail.com from any address with the subject Delete my Seven Keep account, and tell us the Google account you signed in with or, for a guest account, your display name and roughly when you last played. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm by email.
