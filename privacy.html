Seven Keep — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

Seven Keep ("the game") is made by The Gate Keeper ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android version of Seven Keep.

In short: the game gives every installation an anonymous guest account, with no name, email or password, so that you can play online and, if you sign in with Google, keep your progress across devices. That account and its data are stored with Unity Gaming Services. You can delete the account and its data at any time from the game (section 5a). The game is free and shows ads, served by Unity LevelPlay. On first launch you choose whether those ads may be personalised and whether GameAnalytics may receive anonymous play data to help us improve the game. If you decline, the ads are not personalised and GameAnalytics is not started; the game plays the same.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device. If you sign in with Google, the same save is also kept in your game account so it can return on another device (section 5a).

WhatWhy
Your progress: level, experience, coins, gems, Energy, cards and cosmetics you own, orders, campaign and Gauntlet progress, achievements, statisticsSo you keep your progress between sessions
The names you type into your friends listTo show your friends list
Settings: language, sound, tutorial tips seen, your last match setupTo remember your choices
The identifiers of purchases already deliveredSo a purchase is never delivered twice
How many rewarded ads you watched todayTo apply the daily limits on ad rewards

Uninstalling the game deletes this information. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may copy the save file to your own Google Drive so that your progress returns after a reinstall. That backup belongs to your Google account and is handled by Google; we have no access to it.

2. Your choice on first launch

The first time you open the game, a card asks whether you allow personalised ads and analytics. Nothing is sent to GameAnalytics or Unity LevelPlay before you answer.

  • Allow: GameAnalytics is started, and the ads may be personalised.
  • No thanks: GameAnalytics is not started. Ads are still shown, but the ad services are told you did not consent, so they are not personalised (contextual ads only). For players in the United States this is also a request not to sell or share personal information for advertising.

You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Personalised ads and analytics.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the ad choice is asked first by Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform, an IAB TCF consent tool), before any ad network starts. It lets you accept, refuse, or choose purpose by purpose and partner by partner; the ad networks follow your answer, and if you refuse, they show only non-personalised or limited ads. The game's own card then asks only about analytics, and Settings → Ad privacy choices opens Google's form again. Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

3. Analytics (GameAnalytics), only if you allow it

We use GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS, Denmark) to understand how the game is played: for example, which modes are played, how long duels last, where players stop, and how the in-game economy behaves. This helps us balance and fix the game.

GameAnalytics receives:

  • game events: session start and length, duels started and finished with their mode, deck and result, level reached, Energy running out, rewarded ads offered and watched, purchases made (the product, not your payment details), tutorial steps;
  • device information: device model, operating system and version, app version, language, and an approximate country derived from your IP address;
  • identifiers: a random identifier created by GameAnalytics for this installation and, where Android allows it, your device's advertising ID.

GameAnalytics privacy policy: gameanalytics.com/privacy

3a. Crash reports (Unity), for every player

When the game crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report to Unity Cloud Diagnostics (Unity Technologies): the technical description of the error, the last few lines the game logged before it, the device model, the operating system and its version, the app version, and a random identifier Unity creates for this installation. It contains no advertising ID and is never used for ads or marketing.

We use crash reports only to find and fix what breaks the game. This is sent whatever you answered on first launch, because a game that crashes cannot be fixed without it; our legal basis is our legitimate interest in keeping the game working. You can object at any time by contacting us (section 11). Unity keeps crash reports for the period its own privacy policy gives.

4. Ads (Unity LevelPlay)

The game shows two kinds of ads:

  • rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one (for example "Watch Ad · +2 Energy"); a rewarded ad is never required to play;
  • an occasional ad between duels, as you leave the results screen: never during a duel, never in your first days of play, at most one every few duels and never two within ten minutes. Buying Remove Ads turns these off; rewarded ads stay available because you choose them.

Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies), which passes the request to the ad networks that bid for it: Unity Ads, ironSource Ads (both Unity Technologies) and Google AdMob (Google). To serve and measure ads, and to limit how often you see the same ad and prevent fraud, these services may receive the data below. They use it to personalise ads only if you allowed it (section 2).

  • your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, language and approximate location derived from the IP address;
  • information about the ads shown and your interaction with them (viewed, completed, closed).

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Unity privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy · how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

The game is also built with the Unity engine, which may send basic, non-identifying technical information about your device and the game's performance to Unity Technologies.

5a. Your game account and online play (Unity Gaming Services)

The game uses Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) as its server. On first launch it creates an anonymous guest account: a random player identifier, with no name, email or password. It is used to:

  • play online: the online duels, the matchmaking queue, a hidden skill rating per queue (used only to find fair opponents; there are no public leaderboards) and your last 20 online results. Your opponents see the display name you choose, your level and your avatar. When no other player is found within a few seconds, your opponent is played by the game: a computer opponent with its own display name;
  • keep your progress across devices, if you sign in with Google: a copy of your save (the same information as in section 1) and a short summary of it (level, duels played, last played, device model);
  • keep time for the season: the server's date decides the season's day.

Reporting a name: the Report button (on a match in Profile → History, and in an online duel's Settings) opens your own email app with a message to us that names the reported display name, the match and your player identifier. You decide whether to send it. We use it only to review the name and, if needed, rename or remove that player, and we delete it once handled.

Deleting your account: in Settings → Account → Delete account. This deletes, on our servers, your save, your online results and ratings, your player identifier and the account itself, and resets the game on your device to a new guest. It cannot be undone. A duel in progress is counted as a loss. If you no longer have the game, see how to delete your account without the game.

Unity privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

5. Purchases (Google Play)

Gem bundles, the Welcome bundle, Remove Ads, the Royal Ledger, the Banner Pack and the coffers (and, in later seasons, the Crown Pass and the Royal Crown) are sold through Google Play. Every purchase is a one-time purchase: the game sells no subscription and nothing renews automatically. Your payment details are handled by Google and never reach us or the game. The game receives only what is needed to deliver the purchase (the product and a purchase receipt). Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

6. What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts. The guest account (section 5a) holds none of them.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

7. Children

Seven Keep is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the game and shared information through the services above, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Union or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent at any time (Settings → Personalised ads and analytics). You can delete your game account and its data yourself (section 5a); for anything else, contact us and we will act on it or forward the request to GameAnalytics or Unity as needed. You can also contact them directly through the links above.

9. Security and retention

Information is sent over encrypted connections. Information on your device stays there until you uninstall the game or delete your account. Your game account's data stays on Unity Gaming Services until you delete the account. GameAnalytics and Unity keep the analytics and ad data they receive for the periods described in their own privacy policies.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
